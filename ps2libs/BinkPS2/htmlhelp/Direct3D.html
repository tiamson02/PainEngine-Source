Direct3D

Discussion

Direct3D is a Microsoft supplied 3D graphics API. It is used to drive your 3D graphics card.

You can use Direct3D to do fast alpha-plane controlled transparent video playback (see "ExamAlph.C" and "DX9RAD3D.CPP" or "DX8RAD3D.CPP" for a DirectX 8 example).

You will usually Direct3D to display your video frames on the Xbox, since it can render right out of the YUV textures. The Xbox supports linear, non-power-of-2 textures, and pixel shaders, so this is fast and super-high quality.

You will always Direct3D to display your video frames on the Xenon (there is no overlay support yet). The Xenon supports linear, non-power-of-2 textures, and pixel shaders, so this is fast and super-high quality.

You can read more about Direct3D at the Microsoft DirectX website.
